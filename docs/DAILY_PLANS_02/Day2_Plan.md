## 3. GLAROKAVOS BEACH

Location: near Pefkochori.

Purpose:
Swimming / beach / relaxation.

Characteristics:

* Clear water
* Sandy beach with some pebbled sections
* Organized sections available
* Sunbeds / umbrellas available in parts
* Food and drinks available near the organized beach area
* Parking available in informal areas near beach access

IMPORTANT:

**Use the OPEN-SEA SIDE / SEA SIDE for swimming — NOT the lagoon side.**

The open-sea side provides better conditions for proper swimming.

When parking, prefer access from the SEA SIDE to minimize unnecessary walking.

Possible beach-bar facilities may include:

* Toilets
* Showers
* Changing facilities
* Food / drinks

Sunbed or minimum-consumption conditions may vary and should be checked locally.

Status: APPROVED
