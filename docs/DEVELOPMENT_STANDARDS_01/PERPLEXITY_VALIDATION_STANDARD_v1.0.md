# KASSANDRA-2026
# PERPLEXITY VALIDATION STANDARD

Version: 1.0
Status: ACTIVE
Project Owner: URA

---

# 1. PURPOSE

This document defines the use of Perplexity as an additional external validation source for the Kassandra-2026 project.

Perplexity is not the single source of truth.

Its purpose is to:

- Perform additional external verification
- Find current information
- Identify conflicts between sources
- Discover additional sources
- Improve the reliability of information used in the application

---

# 2. ROLE OF PERPLEXITY

Perplexity shall serve as an:

EXTERNAL VALIDATION LAYER

Workflow:

ChatGPT / Research
↓
Primary Sources
↓
Perplexity Review
↓
Cross-Check
↓
Final Approval

No decision shall be accepted solely because Perplexity produced a particular answer.

---

# 3. VALIDATION SCOPE

Perplexity may be used to validate:

## Businesses and Places

- Restaurants
- Cafes
- Bakeries
- Gelaterias
- Shops
- Supermarkets
- Wineries
- Honey producers
- Olive oil producers
- Attractions
- Beaches
- Local services

## Business Information

- Business name
- Address
- Location
- Opening hours
- Operating days
- Telephone
- Official website
- Official pages
- Current operating status

## Quality Validation

- Quality of the place
- Suitability for the "Best of the Best" standard
- Reputation
- Recent reviews
- Character of the place
- Suitability for the trip
- Whether the place is excessively tourist-oriented
- Whether a better alternative exists nearby

## Maps and Navigation

- Geographic location
- Correct address
- Map location
- Navigation destination
- Consistency between available mapping sources

## Routes

- Logical order of stops
- Distances between stops
- Estimated driving times
- Significant route deviations
- Known route problems when available

---

# 4. PRIMARY SOURCE PRIORITY

Whenever an official source exists, it receives priority.

Preferred order:

1. Official business/place website
2. Google Maps / Google Business
3. Official municipal/tourism authority
4. Official business social page
5. Other reliable sources
6. Perplexity Review

Perplexity supplements official sources; it does not replace them.

---

# 5. BUSINESS VALIDATION PROCEDURE

Before a business or place is finally added to an itinerary:

1. Identify the exact business/place.
2. Verify that it is currently operating.
3. Verify the address.
4. Verify the map location.
5. Check opening days and hours.
6. Evaluate quality and suitability.
7. Perform Perplexity Review.
8. Compare Perplexity findings with other sources.
9. Resolve significant conflicts.
10. Obtain final approval.

---

# 6. BEST OF THE BEST REVIEW

For every significant place, determine whether it genuinely meets the:

BEST OF THE BEST

standard.

Review questions include:

- Is the place considered particularly good?
- Are recent reviews consistently positive?
- Are there recurring significant complaints?
- Does it suit the character of the trip?
- Is there a clearly better alternative nearby?
- Has it become excessively tourist-oriented?
- Is there evidence of local use or reputation?
- Is it worth the required travel?

---

# 7. MAP VALIDATION

A written address alone is insufficient.

Verify:

Business Name
↓
Address
↓
Map Pin
↓
Navigation Destination

If the business address and map location conflict:

STOP.

Do not approve the location until the conflict is resolved.

---

# 8. ROUTE VALIDATION

Before approving a travel day, review the sequence of stops.

For significant movements verify:

- Starting point
- Destination
- Distance
- Estimated travel time
- Geographic logic
- Route deviation
- Parking considerations when relevant

Perplexity may be used to identify potential route issues.

Actual navigation shall use the mapping/navigation services approved by the project.

---

# 9. CONFLICT RESOLUTION

If sources conflict:

DO NOT GUESS.

Mark the issue:

VALIDATION CONFLICT

Use additional evidence to resolve it.

Example:

Official Website: Open Monday
Google Maps: Closed Monday
Perplexity: Open Monday

Result:

Opening on Monday is NOT considered verified.

Additional verification is required.

---

# 10. UNCERTAIN INFORMATION

When sufficient certainty cannot be achieved, mark the information:

UNVERIFIED

Unverified information must not be presented as confirmed fact.

---

# 11. TIME-SENSITIVE INFORMATION

Information that can change must be rechecked closer to the travel date.

Examples:

- Opening hours
- Operating days
- Seasonal closures
- Business closures
- Parking conditions
- Road works
- Traffic restrictions

Previous verification does not guarantee future availability.

---

# 12. DAY CONTENT REVIEW

After the content of a travel day has been prepared and before final approval, perform:

PERPLEXITY DAY REVIEW

The review shall examine:

- Quality of selected places
- Suitability of selected places
- Potentially better alternatives
- Possible problems
- Missing information
- Conflicting information
- Map/location concerns
- Route concerns

Only after validation and Project Owner approval may the day's content be marked:

FINAL APPROVED

---

# 13. APPROVED CONTENT FREEZE

After a place has passed:

Research
+
Primary Source Verification
+
Perplexity Review
+
Project Owner Approval

it becomes:

APPROVED

An approved selection shall not be reopened without a concrete reason such as:

- New information
- Business closure
- Significant change
- Route problem
- Validation conflict
- Project Owner request

---

# 14. RESPONSIBILITY

ChatGPT manages the validation process.

Responsibilities include:

- Identifying when validation is required
- Performing initial research
- Defining what Perplexity should verify
- Comparing results
- Identifying conflicts
- Clearly stating uncertainty
- Presenting findings to the Project Owner

Perplexity serves as an external validation advisor only.

---

# 15. CORE RULE

PERPLEXITY DOES NOT DECIDE.

PERPLEXITY VALIDATES.

Final decisions are based on:

Facts
+
Official Sources
+
Cross-Verification
+
Project Requirements
+
Project Owner Approval
